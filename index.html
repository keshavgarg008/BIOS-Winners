About Gaon Setu

Gaon Setu is an offline-first child welfare record management app that helps clinics, community health workers, and schools maintain and access children's records without internet connectivity. Each device stores records locally, allowing users to continue working independently. When devices reconnect, changes are synchronized and safely merged ensuring that no important information is silently lost.
